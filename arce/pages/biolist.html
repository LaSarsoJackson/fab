Mayors of Albany Tour - Former Mayors of Albany.

  • Johannes Abeel: Merchant, trader, alderman and Judge.
  • Johannes Jacobse Beeckman: Also Firemaster, NYS Assembly.
  • Charles Edward Bleecker: Planned construction of Washhington Park, first Mayor to have veto power.
  • Rutger Bleecker: Enacted laws restricting the sale of intoxicants to indians.
  • Johannes Bleecker, Jr.: Served as Indian interpreter, carried captive to Canada 1686.
  • Jan Jansen Bleeker: Born in Holand, also served as Justice of the Peace.
  • James Henry Blessing: Albany's first Public Baths were opened during his term, First mayor of the "Barnes" machine.
  • Dirck Ten Broeck: Also served as Indian commissioner and militia Captain.
  • John G. Burch: Appointed to serve as mayor during contested election between Thacher & Judson.
  • Erastus Corning: Founder of the New York Central Railroad and member of the United States House of Representatives.
  • Erastus Corning II: Albany's longest serving Mayor.
  • Johannes Cuyler: Elder in the Dutch Church, Indian commissioner, trader.
  • Johannes DePeyster: Also former Mayor of NYC, purchased Albany's first fire fighting equipment.
  • Volkert Petrus Douw: Merchant and one of the founders of Albany Savings Bank.
  • Charles E. Dudley: Albany basin of the Erie Canal completed during his term, Dudley Observatory namesake.
  • Charles H. Gaus: Much labor unrest during his term, resigned to become NYS Comptroller.
  • Charles Watson Godard: Lecturer, philanthropist, Captain of the Port of NY.
  • William Stormont Hackett: First Mayor elected under the "Dan O'Connell" machine.
  • Hendrick Hansen: Also Trader, merchant, alderman & Indian Commissioner.
  • Frank Salisbury Harris: Actimg Mayor during Erastus Corning II's military service.
  • Herman F. Hoogkamp: Completed the unexpired term of John Boyd Thatcher II.
  • Friend Humphrey: During his term, Albany opened it's first telegraph office & closed public markets on Sundays.
  • Edmund L. Judson: Declared the winner of contested Mayor's race weeks before his first term ended.
  • James Hilton Manning: During his term, Albany's trolleys converted to electric power.
  • James B. McEwan: Also NYS Assembly, Senate and Albany Postmaster.
  • Eli Perry: Merchant in livestock & provisions, Albany Law School organized during his tenure as mayor.
  • Jared Lewis Rathbone: Last mayor chosen by the Common Council, President of Albany Medical College.
  • Johannes Schuyler: Enacted a law that each homeowner construct an 8 foot sidewalk.
  • Myndert Schuyler: Ordered construction of housing for Indians coming to Albany for trade.
  • Pieter Schuyler: First Mayor of Albany
  • Johannes Schuyler Jr: Declined a second term by refusing to take the required oath of allegiance.
  • Henry F. Snyder: Completed the 4th term of Charles Gaus, then became Postmaster of Albany
  • Ambrose Spencer: Also US House of Representitaves, NY Supreme Court & NY Attorneys General.
  • Barent Philip Staats: Physician, strong temperance ideas.
  • Joseph William Stevens: Also served as President of the Common Council.
  • James Stevenson: One of the first governors of Albany City Hospital.
  • John Swinburne: Electric streetlights installed during his term.
  • John Taylor: Brewer, and known for his fine private library.
  • Abraham TenBroeck: In his term, Albany became the capital of NY, whipping posts were aboloshid in the city. Dirck TenBroeck: Grandson of Mayor Dirick Wessele TenBroeck
  • Jacob Coenraedt Ten Eyck: Also Indian commissioner, Judge & silversmith.
  • George Hornell Thacher: Headed Albany's humanitarian efforts duriing the Civil War.
  • John Boyd Thacher: Namesake of John Boyd Thatcher State Park, philonthropist, NYS Senator
  • John Boyd Thacher II: Resigned in 1940 to serve as Judge of Albany County Children's Court
  • Franklin Townsend: Adjutant General of NYS, Assemblyman, Pres. of NYS Savings Bank.
  • John Townsend: Laid the cornerstone of Albany's old City Hall in 1829.
  • Thomas J. Van Alstyne: First public water filtration plant opened during his term.
  • Teunis Van Vechten: First locomotive arrived during his term, also Attorney and Albany Insurance Co, President.
  • Goose VanSchaick: His administration witnessed some of the most active local phases of the French & Indian War
  • James R. Watt: Last Mayor of the "Barnes" machine, First elected mayor where women voted.
  • Oren Elbridge Wilson: Appointed a Water Board which later led to Albany's first Filtration Plant, Increased size of the Fire Department.
  • Abraham Yates, Jr.: Continental Congress delegate, Albany's first Postmaster, Installation of Oil street lamps during his term.
