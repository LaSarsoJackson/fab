Search by section, lot or tier

Follow the screenshots, then try it in Burial Locator.

Open Burial Locator

Read the four steps below.

  1. Step 1

    Choose the location fields

    Open Burial Locator. Use the Section, Lot and Tier fields under Location. You can leave Name blank.

  2. Step 2

    Enter the location

    This example uses Section 215, Lot 30. Leave Tier blank for a lot search. For a tier, enter Section and Tier instead. Results update as you type.

  3. Step 3

    Select a burial

    Check the names, dates and locations in the results. This example selects Thomas E LaMont. Use Show more results when there are more matches.

  4. Step 4

    Open the grave on the map

    Selecting a result opens its details and pin on Cemetery Map. Choose Directions to plan a route to the selected grave.
