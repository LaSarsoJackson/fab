Search for a burial

Follow the screenshots, then try it in Burial Locator.

Open Burial Locator

Read the four steps below.

  1. Step 1

    Enter a name

    Type all or part of a name in Name. This example uses Thomas LaMont. Add Section, Lot or Tier to narrow the results.

  2. Step 2

    Select the burial

    Compare the name, dates and location. This example selects Thomas E LaMont, Section 215, Lot 30. Use Show more results to see later matches.

  3. Step 3

    Check the map

    The selected record opens on Cemetery Map. Read its location details, then choose Directions to plan a route.

  4. Step 4

    Choose a route start

    Select From to choose your start: a map point, a listed place or your device’s location. To is the selected grave. Follow posted cemetery signs when visiting.
