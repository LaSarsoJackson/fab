App help

Search burial records, explore historical tours, and find your way around the cemetery map.

Open the cemetery map

Find a burial or tour stop

In Burial Locator, enter a name and, if you know it, a cemetery section. Select a result to open its location on Cemetery Map. Records show section, lot, and grave details when available.

In Search Tours, choose a collection and select a stop. Biography links open the ARCE website. Some records have incomplete location information.

Use location and directions

Location access is optional. Choose Directions on a grave or the map. From and To open choices for your current position, a point on the map, or a mapped place from a list. Start from my location uses one position fix. Follow my location updates the route as you walk; Stop following ends those direction updates. The map’s location button works separately.

If location is blocked, check Location Services and the app or browser permission in your device settings. You can choose a route start on the map or from the list instead. For keyboard map picking, use the arrow keys to pan, then confirm the point at the crosshair.

Mapped routes follow the available cemetery-road data. They do not confirm current access, surface conditions, or accessibility. Follow posted rules and check conditions on site.

If the map does not load

The app needs an internet connection to load maps, records, and images. Check your connection, then retry or reopen the app. On iPhone, the Home button returns the current tab to its start page.

Report a problem

Email geo.jrk1@gmail.com for app support or to report a record or map issue. Include the app version, device model, what you tried, and what happened. For a record correction, include the person's name and cemetery section if known.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, or information you do not want included in your support message. Screenshots are optional; remove personal details before sending them.

This contact handles the app. For cemetery services, access arrangements, or official burial documentation, contact Albany Rural Cemetery directly.
