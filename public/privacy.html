Privacy policy

Updated September 27, 2026

Albany Grave Finder provides cemetery maps, burial search, and historical tours. GeoJRK maintains the app. This policy covers iOS version 1.0.3 and the FAB website.

You do not need an account. The app does not include advertising or a separate analytics service. It loads content from hosting and map providers that receive network requests from your device, as described below.

Location and directions

If you allow location access and use a location control, the app uses your device's position to show where you are near the cemetery or calculate a route along mapped cemetery roads. That route calculation runs on your device. The app does not upload a GPS history or run a background location service.

You can decline location access or turn it off in your device settings. Search, tours, and routes with a start point selected on the map remain available.

When you choose to open directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps, the link includes the destination and, when selected, a route origin. That origin may be your current position. The maps provider then handles those details under its own privacy policy.

Search and saved settings

Burial searches run against an index downloaded to your device. The app does not send the names you type to a search service. Map preferences and your last selected tour stop are stored locally so the app can restore them. They are not synced to an app account.

Your browser or the iOS app may cache downloaded files. Browser site-data controls can clear web settings and cached content. Deleting the iOS app removes its local app data; a device backup may retain a copy.

Hosting, map tiles, and images

The web experience and burial index are hosted on GitHub Pages. Street tiles come from OpenStreetMap, terrain tiles from Esri, and tour images from the ARCE website hosted by the University at Albany. Each provider receives requests when its content loads, including your IP address and request details such as the requested file or map tile and browser information. A requested tile identifies the area being viewed; it does not by itself establish that you are there.

GitHub states that Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. OpenStreetMap and Esri describe server logs and service operations in their privacy policies. Provider retention periods and uses are governed by those policies; the app does not control their logs.

Biography and other external links open a separate website or app. Its privacy practices apply after you open it. Apple also processes App Store and TestFlight information under its own policies.

Support messages

If you email app support, your message includes your email address and any details or attachments you choose to send. Those details are used to respond and investigate the issue. A support conversation may be retained as a record of the issue and response. You can ask for deletion of your support correspondence; this does not remove records held independently by hosting, email, or map providers.

Contact

Earlier iOS versions, including 1.0.2, display the older ARCE website hosted by the University at Albany. Their hosted content and network requests are governed by the University at Albany internet privacy policy. The local routing and saved-setting details above describe the updated app.

For privacy questions or requests about app support correspondence, email geo.jrk1@gmail.com. Include enough detail to identify the request, but do not send passwords or verification codes.

Changes to the app's data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
