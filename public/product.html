Before you begin

Know what the tool can tell you.

Is the route preview turn-by-turn navigation?

It follows mapped cemetery roads. It does not guarantee an accessible path, entrance availability, or current conditions; check the route on site.

Where can I learn about the project?

The Albany Rural Cemetery Explorer website provides the project’s historical and academic context. The app links to that site.
